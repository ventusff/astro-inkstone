## Callout

Five variants; `title` replaces the variant's default label (Note / Intuition / Warning / Important / Abstract — the same defaults the quote syntax uses, localizable through `labels`). The [[kitchen-sink]] shows the quote-syntax and raw-HTML spellings of the same markup.

<Callout variant="intuition" title="When to reach for the component">
  In MDX, when the content is authored inline. The quote syntax covers plain-Markdown files; both render identical markup.
</Callout>

## Chip

Inline badges for status and categories, in the four variants `PaperCard` tags and `HubCard` chips share: <Chip variant="teal">stable</Chip> <Chip variant="mustard">beta</Chip> <Chip variant="violet">research</Chip> <Chip variant="orange">new</Chip> — or a custom tone for topic palettes: <Chip tone={{ bg: '#e8f0ee', fg: '#1f5f56' }}>custom tone</Chip>

## Steps

A markdown list on a vertical rail:

<Steps>
1. Add the two submodules and the `file:` dependencies.
2. Import `tokens.css`, `base.css` and (for a wiki) `browse.css` in your global stylesheet.
3. Wire `siteMarkdown()` into `astro.config`.
4. Override the tier-one palette with your identity pigments.
</Steps>

## PaperCard

The survey workhorse — alias, full title, venue tags, author meta, links, and two dedicated slots:

<PaperCard
  alias="Transformer"
  full="Attention Is All You Need"
  tags={[{ label: 'NeurIPS 2017', variant: 'teal' }]}
  meta="Vaswani, Shazeer, Parmar, et al."
  links={[{ label: 'arXiv:1706.03762', href: 'https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762' }]}
  id="transformer-card"
>
  <Fragment slot="pain">Recurrent models process tokens one after another — long-range dependencies fade, and nothing parallelizes.</Fragment>
  **Key idea**: attention alone, no recurrence — every token attends to every other in one step, and the whole sequence trains in parallel.
  <Fragment slot="diff">The architecture nearly every large language model since has built on.</Fragment>
</PaperCard>

Under a deep-dive heading, a one-line plain-language gloss (`.speak`) tells the reader what a module *does* before the mechanism:

<p class="speak">Attention is a soft lookup table: every token asks "who is relevant to me?" and gets a weighted mix of the answers.</p>
